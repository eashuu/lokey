# Code of Conduct

lokey follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure.
- Respect that people have different tools, time zones and languages.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through a GitHub security advisory contact or by emailing the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
